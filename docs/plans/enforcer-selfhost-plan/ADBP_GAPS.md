## DOCTRINE (tier legend)

Enforcement must be **mechanical + TESTED**. Every gap below is classified onto the three-rung ladder:

- **T1 — deterministic / blocking.** Ships a **fail-fixture** (must be flagged) + **pass-fixture** (must stay clean) + a **detection test** over both. Anything that blocks must be T1.
- **T2 — scored / advisory (non-blocking).** A regex/AST/heuristic emitting a `score`+`confidence`; fixtures assert the score crosses (fail) or stays under (pass) the threshold. Mechanized, tested, but not fatal.
- **T3 — justified prose.** Reserved for genuine judgment where even a scored test is impossible. Does NOT count as enforcement; must carry the label `advisory, no mechanization possible + <reason>` — and the *presence of that label* is itself T1-enforced.

**Dedup rule:** rows repeated across source files (e.g. "≤5 params" appears in Python, Rust, Flutter, CFML, agents-skills; "FSM for stateful entity" appears in 5 files) are collapsed to **one** row here, with the union of stacks/sources noted. Per-language variants of a *cross-cutting* gap are collapsed; genuinely language-specific gaps (Dart secure-storage, CFML `cfqueryparam`, etc.) stay as their own rows under the New languages group.

Columns match the source gap files (`ADBP point` · `ADBP source` · `Backed?` · `Tier` · `Proposed ruleId`(s) · `Fail-fixture` · `Pass-fixture`) plus a **`Maps to new workpack`** column proposing a Track-D or Track-E workpack id.
